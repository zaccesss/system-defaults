# Support

Need help or have a question?

- **Questions and ideas** - open a discussion or an issue.
- **Something broken?** - open an issue with the operating system, the script and what
  happened. See [guides/reference.md](guides/reference.md) first, the behaviour might be
  intentional.

> [!TIP]
> Reach me directly at support@isaacadjei.me for help. For anything else, use
> contact@isaacadjei.me or my [contact page](https://isaacadjei.me/contact).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For a security issue, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than posting publicly.
